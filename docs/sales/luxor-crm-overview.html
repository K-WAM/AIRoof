From the first leak call to the final invoice, each handoff has a clear next step.

Your office confirms bookings, creates jobs and reviews documents before sending.

  1. 1

    The call gets answered.

    AI answers 24/7 in English and Spanish, captures the request and books an inspection time for office confirmation.

  2. 2

    The visit gets scheduled.

    Review the request, confirm it and create a job. Assign a crew on the calendar.

  3. 3

    The crew records the site.

    Open the QR field link. Add voice notes, photos, findings and time; the office sees updates as they sync.

  4. 4

    The customer sees the work.

    Build a quote from findings for online acceptance. Follow with a price-free report and selected photos.

  5. 5

    The office closes the loop.

    Review and send the invoice. Record payments received, track the balance and enable overdue reminders.
