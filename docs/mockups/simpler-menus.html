Simpler menus: proposals for your approval

Nothing here changes the app until you approve it. For the November 13 review the app only adds pages; every proposal below is built afterwards, one at a time, each with its own approval.

Rule used: what a person does every day stays one tap away; everything else moves inside the page it belongs to. No feature is removed.

M5. A job goes from 8 tabs to 3 bigger change, after the review

Today a job shows Activity, Photos, Materials, Labor and then ① Findings ② Quote ③ Report ④ Invoice. People look for documents in two rows of tabs.

Work = field notes, materials and labor as three short sections of one log.
