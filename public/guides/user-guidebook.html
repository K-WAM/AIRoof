Your first day

Set up the shared details before your team starts work. Your plan and user type decide which screens you see.

Set up in this order

  1. Open Settings. Check the business name, contact details, time zone and hours. Enter the hours when you can take bookings.
  2. Check your phone line and call handling with your administrator. Make a test call and check the result in Calls and Pipeline.
  3. In Library → Logo, upload your logo. Check how it appears on a document.
  4. In Library → Prices & rates, enter the materials, units, prices and labor rates you use. Check each amount before using it on a quote or invoice.
  5. In the work catalog, enter the work you offer and its details. Keep work descriptions separate from material prices.
  6. Add each crew or provider in Library. Add the people on each crew. Calendar rows use these crews or providers.
Done right looks like: A test call appears, hours match your real schedule, and a sample document has the right logo and prices.

Common mistakes: Leaving old hours in place, treating example prices as your own, or using the wrong unit.

Finish setup

People and customers

  1. Open Team if you are an admin. Invite each person separately and choose the access they need: Admin, Office staff, Inspector, Technician or View only.
  2. Use the field link for a crew member who only needs a single job. They do not need an account for that link.
  3. Open Customers. Add or check each customer's name, phone, email and address. Search first to avoid a duplicate.
  4. In Settings → Getting paid, check payment details if your plan includes Billing.
  5. Have each person sign in and check that they can reach the screens needed for their work.
Done right looks like: Staff can find the right customer, field workers can open their job, and each person has only the access needed.

Common mistakes: Sharing a login, giving everyone admin access, or entering an old customer email.

If you book appointments

Your Calendar may show bookings and providers instead of jobs and crews. Drag the booking to a provider and day, then confirm it. Some set-price businesses do not have Findings or Quote. Screen and customer names can differ by business.

Use the sections that appear in your plan. For more field detail, see the Field Operations Guide.

Dashboard, Calls & Pipeline

Dashboard

What it is for: A starting view of calls, bookings and work needing attention.

How to use it:

  1. Open Dashboard each morning.
  2. Open an item that needs attention and review its details.

Daily SOP: Check new activity and hand off anything you cannot resolve.

Common mistake: Assuming a request is confirmed because it appears on the Dashboard.

Calls

What it is for: Review calls answered by the phone AI.

  1. Open Calls and choose a call.
  2. Read the summary or play the call. Check the caller's needs and contact details.

Daily SOP: Review new calls and follow up on missed or unclear details.

Common mistake: Relying on a summary when the recording needs a closer check.

Pipeline

What it is for: Review new requests and bookings.

  1. Open Pipeline and read each new request. Use Call Back if details need checking.
  2. Choose Confirm or Decline. Confirm sends the customer a confirmation; Decline lets them know.
  3. Where jobs are enabled, use Create Job when the request needs a job.

Daily SOP: Work through new requests and check that each has a clear next step.

Common mistake: Promising a time before checking and confirming the request.

Calendar & Jobs

Calendar

What it is for: Place work on a day and assign a crew or provider.

  1. Open Calendar and find the unscheduled job or booking.
  2. Drag it to the correct crew or provider and day. Pick the time.
  3. For jobs, review the details and choose Confirm + email crew.

Daily SOP: Review today's schedule and resolve conflicts with the office.

Common mistake: Dropping work on the wrong row or day without checking the time.

Jobs

What it is for: Keep the customer, work, field updates and documents together.

  1. Open Jobs and choose the right job. Check the customer, address and scope.
  2. Review Findings and record what was found. Use those details to prepare a Quote, if your business uses quotes.
  3. Review the Photos and Report. Make sure the work and images match this job.
  4. Review the Invoice, amounts and recipient. Check each document before you send it.
  5. Use Send to a worker to send the field link when the worker needs access.

Daily SOP: Check active jobs for new notes, photos and documents waiting for review.

Common mistakes: Sending a draft without checking it, or using a field link for the wrong job.

Field & Library

Field

What it is for: Record time, work and photos from the site.

  1. Open Field and pick today's job, or open the link from the office. Check the job name.
  2. Tap Clock in when you arrive. Use the time controls for lunch and leaving.
  3. Use Hold to talk, or tap to start to say what you did and used. Type a note if it is noisy or there is no signal.
  4. Use + Photo for before and after photos. Add a short description.
  5. Tap Clock out for the day when finished.

Daily SOP: Check the job, record work as it happens, and tell the office about anything urgent.

Common mistake: Recording a note on the wrong job. If you correct a number, confirm the change.

Library

What it is for: Maintain the shared details used by your team and documents.

  1. Open Library and choose the area you need: prices and rates, work catalog, crews or logo.
  2. Enter clear names, units and current amounts. Check the saved details before others use them.

Daily SOP: Check changes requested by the team; keep prices and crew details current.

Common mistakes: Confusing work descriptions with prices, or leaving an outdated material price in use.

Billing & Settings

Billing

What it is for: See who has paid and who is late.

  1. Open Billing and review outstanding balances.
  2. Open the related invoice or job to check the customer, amount and status.
  3. Resolve any mismatch with the office before contacting the customer.

Daily SOP: Review overdue balances and payment activity. Payment reminders go out on their own.

Common mistake: Treating a draft invoice as a sent invoice. Review and send the invoice from the job.

Settings

What it is for: Keep business contact details, hours and payment setup current.

  1. Open Settings and check business details and hours.
  2. Update hours when they change. The phone AI books only inside those hours.
  3. Open Getting paid when available and check payment details.
  4. Check the saved values and tell the team about changes that affect their work.

Daily SOP: Check for changes to hours or contact details; update them as soon as they are known.

Common mistake: Changing hours without checking the day and time zone.

Need help? Open Guide and choose Ask a question. Describe the screen and what you were trying to do.

Daily SOP · Admin / owner

Keep the business ready to take work and get paid.

  1. Open Dashboard. Review new activity and items that need your attention.
  2. Check Calls and Pipeline. Make sure new requests have an owner and a next step.
  3. Review Calendar for gaps or conflicts. Ask the office to resolve them.
  4. Check Billing for unpaid and late invoices. Review any unusual balance.
  5. Keep Settings current, especially hours, contact details and Getting paid.
  6. Review Library prices, work catalog, crews and logo when they change.
  7. In Team, invite each person with the access needed for their role. Remove access when it is no longer needed.
Before you finish: Make sure urgent requests have been handed to a named person.

Avoid: Sharing accounts, leaving old hours active, or assuming a customer has been contacted before a confirmation is sent.

Daily SOP · Office staff

Move each request toward a clear next step.

  1. Open Dashboard and Pipeline. Read new requests and check the customer's contact details.
  2. Use Call Back when a detail is missing or unclear.
  3. Confirm or Decline each request after checking the details. Confirm sends the customer a confirmation.
  4. When work needs a job, use Create Job from the request. Check the name, phone and address carried over.
  5. Open Calendar. Place the job on the right crew and day, pick the time, and choose Confirm + email crew.
  6. Open the job and use Send to a worker when a field link is needed.
  7. Review findings, photos, quote, report and invoice as available. Check the recipient and contents before sending.
  8. Search Customers before adding a new record. Update details when the customer gives a correction.
Before you finish: Hand off unresolved requests and check tomorrow's schedule.

Avoid: Creating a duplicate customer or treating an unconfirmed request as a promise.

Daily SOP · Inspector / Technician

Capture clear facts so the office can decide what happens next.

  1. Open Field or the job link from the office. Check the customer, job name and address.
  2. Clock in when you arrive. Follow your team's time process for breaks and leaving.
  3. Inspect the work. Record what you found in plain words and include measurements when useful.
  4. Use the talk control or type a note. Check that the note belongs to this job.
  5. Add before and after photos with short descriptions where useful.
  6. If your access and workflow include Findings or Quote, review the details and prepare them for the office.
  7. Correct any wrong number and confirm the correction. Tell the office about urgent issues.
  8. Clock out for the day when finished.
Before you finish: Check that the office has the facts and photos needed to follow up.

Avoid: Guessing at a measurement or sending a customer document without the required review.

Daily SOP · Crew field link

Your link opens the job the office sent. No account is needed.

  1. Open the link from the office on your phone. Check the job name and address.
  2. Tap Clock in when you arrive.
  3. Use Hold to talk, or tap to start. Say what you did and what you used. Spanish is fine.
  4. If it is noisy or there is no signal, type your note instead.
  5. Tap + Photo to add before and after photos. Add a short description.
  6. Use the time controls for lunch and leaving. Tap Clock out for the day when finished.
  7. If you say a wrong number, say the correction and confirm the change.
Before you finish: Check your notes and photos and tell the office about urgent problems.

Avoid: Using a link for a different job. Ask the office for the right link if the name or address is wrong.

For more detail, open the Field Operations Guide.

Daily SOP · View only

Use the information you can see to stay informed. Ask an authorized teammate to make changes.

  1. Open Dashboard and review the items relevant to your work.
  2. Open a call, request, customer or job that you need to understand, if your plan and access show it.
  3. Read the available details and check the date and status.
  4. Tell the office or an admin if a detail looks wrong or needs action.
  5. Ask an authorized person to confirm a request, edit a record or send a document.
Before you finish: Pass along anything urgent with the customer or job name so it can be found quickly.

Avoid: Assuming that seeing a document means it has been sent or a request has been confirmed.

Quick answers

  1. Where do new requests go?
    Open Pipeline. Review and Confirm or Decline them.
  2. Is a booking confirmed automatically?
    The office reviews it and chooses Confirm. A confirmation is then sent to the customer.
  3. Where can I hear a call?
    Open Calls, choose the call and play it or read its summary.
  4. How do I schedule work?
    In Calendar, drag the job or booking to the right row and day, choose the time and confirm.
  5. How does a worker get the job?
    On the job, use Send to a worker. The field link needs no account.
  6. Where do photos go?
    Use + Photo in Field. Check the job's Photos before sending a report.
  7. Where do prices come from?
    Library → Prices & rates. Check them on each quote and invoice.
  8. Can I send a document without checking it?
    Review its contents and recipient first, then send it from the job.
  9. Why is a screen missing?
    Your plan, industry or user type controls what you can see. Ask your admin.
  10. How do I get help?
    Open Guide and choose Ask a question. Include the screen and the task.
