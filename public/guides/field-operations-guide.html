Section 2

Field Worker Guide

Give this to your foremen and crews. 3 steps, under 2 minutes to learn.

What you need A smartphone with Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone). No app install. Just open the link.
1
Open the Field screen
Your office will share a link or QR code. Open it in Chrome or Safari. Bookmark it to your home screen so it's one tap away next time.
Android: tap ⋮ → "Add to Home Screen"  |  iPhone: tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
2
Select your job
Tap the dropdown and find your job number (e.g. J-1042). The address and client name appear below so you can confirm it's the right one. Optionally enter your name.
3
Hold the mic button and speak your update
Press and hold the big microphone button. Speak naturally — say what you did, who's there, what materials you used, any problems. Release when done — it submits automatically. No submit button. No extra taps. You'll see "✓ Logged" when it's saved.
Works on iOS and Android. The app works in any language — just speak normally in your phone's language.

What to say — examples

English "Job 1042, we arrived at 8:30 AM. Kevin and Miguel are on site. We pulled 40 squares of old shingles and found rotted decking on the northeast corner — about 6 sheets need replacing."
Spanish "Trabajo 1042. Llegamos a las nueve. Usamos veinte láminas de metal y encontramos una filtración cerca del canalón. Necesitamos más sellador mañana."
Any language — casual is fine "We finished up around 3, customer signed off, took photos. Used about 15 tubes of caulk and a half roll of membrane flashing."
Tip Mention times, names, materials with quantities, and any problems. The more detail you give, the more accurate the invoice will be.
Section 3

Office Admin Guide

Create jobs, review field updates, generate reports and invoices.

Creating a Job from an Appointment

1
Go to Appointments
Navigate to Appointments in the left nav. Find the booked inspection.
2
Click "Create Job →"
The form opens pre-filled with the client's name, phone, address, and service type from the call Alice took.
3
Add a job title and click Create
A short job ID is assigned instantly (J-1042). Share this number and the field screen link with your crew.
4
Share the field link with the crew
On the job page, click Copy field link if the crew member already has a portal login, or Field QR if they don't — that shows a QR code (and a plain link as a backup) they can scan with no account needed. The QR code is single-use and expires in 10 minutes, so generate it right when you're handing off the job, not in advance.

Reviewing a Job After Updates Come In

1
Open the Job detail
Go to Jobs → click View → on the job. You'll see all field updates at the bottom and tabs for Timeline, Materials, Labor, Issues.
2
Review structured data by tab
Each tab aggregates everything extracted across all updates. Timeline shows ordered events. Issues are severity-coded red/amber/green.
3
Generate Report or Invoice
Click Generate Report for a plain-text job summary (copy-paste or print). Click Generate Invoice for a draft invoice — edit line items, adjust rates, review totals.
Note on invoice pricing The AI captures quantities and descriptions from what the crew says. Unit prices default to $0 if not mentioned — fill them in before sending to the client.
